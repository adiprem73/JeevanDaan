Schedule your vaccine

This is a static scheduler page that helps users find recommended vaccines and prepare for vaccination. For booking, we link to the Vaccines list or Teleconsult page.

How it works

  1. Review vaccine recommendations below.
  2. Note the recommended dates and prepare documents.
  3. Click "Find nearest center" to view vaccine centers (external).
  4. Use the Teleconsult page for any pre-vaccination consultation.

Recommended Vaccines

  • Influenza (Seasonal) — Annual for adults at-risk.
  • Tdap — Once, then boosters as recommended.
  • COVID-19 — As per current local guidance.
  • MMR — Confirm immunization status.
